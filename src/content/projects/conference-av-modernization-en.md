## Integration at conference scale

Across seven conference environments, the project covered nearly **5,000 devices and conference positions**: around 1,700 delegate and microphone positions, approximately 2,800 listening units, more than 300 AV-over-IP endpoints, and dozens of controlled cameras.

I worked alongside the senior automation developer, who was responsible for the main control-code implementation. I made smaller software changes during integration and focused on device configuration, quality checks, and commissioning.
